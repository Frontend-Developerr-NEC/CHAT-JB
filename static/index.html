Saved personal info

Some facts you share (such as names, preferences, details written as “My … is …”, and a name you assign to the assistant) are saved in this browser and sent to the AI provider with your messages for relevant follow-up answers and suggestions. Facts labeled as passwords, account or card numbers, IDs, or health details are excluded from profile extraction, but this cannot catch every sensitive detail. Your full chat messages are still saved in browser chat history and recent messages are sent to the AI provider. Avoid sharing confidential information; you can remove profile facts here and delete chats from the chat list.
